# AI writing markers in English

Delete or replace with the specific thing meant. Never swap one marker for another from this list.

## Openers
In today's world, In today's fast-paced world, In the ever-evolving landscape of, It's no secret that, It's important to note that, It's worth noting that, Needless to say, Let's dive in, Let's explore, In this article we will, Have you ever wondered, Imagine a world where, Welcome to.

## Emphasis crutches and adverbs
Truly, really, very, genuinely, honestly, incredibly, remarkably, significantly, extremely, absolutely, essentially, basically, fundamentally, ultimately, seamlessly, effortlessly, robust, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, game-changing, groundbreaking, revolutionary, transformative, innovative, powerful, comprehensive, holistic.

## Throat-clearing
Here's the thing, Here's what, The truth is, The reality is, At the end of the day, When it comes to, In terms of, With that said, That being said, Moving forward, Going forward.

## Closers
In conclusion, To sum up, In summary, All in all, Overall, The bottom line is, Ultimately, So there you have it, I hope this helps, Feel free to, Don't hesitate to, Let me know if, Stay tuned, Happy [anything]-ing.

## Structural markers
"Not X, but Y" / "It's not about X, it's about Y", rhetorical question then answer, groups of three, "Whether you're a ... or a ...", "From X to Y", "the key to", "unlock", "harness", "leverage", "empower", "navigate", "landscape", "journey", "ecosystem", "tapestry", "delve", "testament to", "a reminder that", "in a world where".

## Inanimate agency
The data reveals, The decision opens doors, Technology reshapes, The platform empowers you, This feature allows.
Name the person or company doing it.

## Punctuation
No em dashes. No en dashes as separators. No colon before a list in prose. No bold or italics for emphasis. No emoji unless asked.

## Quick checks
Adverb? Cut. Passive? Find the actor. Wh- opener? Restructure. Three matching sentence lengths? Break one. Punchy one-liner closing every paragraph? Vary. Sounds like a pull-quote? Rewrite.
